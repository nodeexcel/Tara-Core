# Tara

> Your personal AI operator on WhatsApp.

Tara is a voice-first AI operator for entrepreneurs. Send a WhatsApp message — text or voice — and Tara takes care of it across your business tools.

---

## What Tara does

- **Talks to you on WhatsApp** — no new app to install, no new login to remember.
- **Understands voice and text** — speak naturally, in your own language.
- **Connects to the tools you already use** — email, calendar, notes, CRM, social, and more.
- **Executes real actions** — drafts, schedules, posts, summarises, follows up.
- **Always asks before doing anything sensitive** — you stay in control.

## Who it's for

Founders, freelancers, coaches, agency owners, and small business operators who'd rather *delegate* repetitive admin than spend their day in dashboards.

## How it works

1. Sign up on the website.
2. Connect your tools in a couple of clicks.
3. Add Tara to your WhatsApp contacts and start chatting.

That's it. No setup calls, no onboarding workshops.
